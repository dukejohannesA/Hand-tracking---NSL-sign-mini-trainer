How to record
  • Hold SPACE to record continuously (about 8 samples per second) — release to stop
  • Press N for next letter, P for previous
  • Vary your hand while recording: tilt it, rotate it slightly, move closer and further, shift left and right. This is what makes the model robust
  • Sign with the back of your hand toward the camera (NSL orientation)
  • Your data is saved in the browser automatically — refreshing won't lose it
  • When all letters hit the goal, click Download JSON
